# iHubMids - Innovation Hub for Machine Intelligence and Data Science

A professional research group website for iHubMids, showcasing the team's expertise in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence.

## Overview

iHubMids is a research group at the University of Europe for Applied Sciences, dedicated to advancing the frontiers of Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence through innovative research and collaboration.

## Features

- **Modern, Responsive Design**: Fully responsive layout that works on all devices
- **Smooth Animations**: Engaging scroll animations and transitions
- **Team Profiles**: Detailed information about all research group members
- **Research Areas**: Showcase of key research domains
- **Publications**: Links to team members' Google Scholar profiles
- **Contact Form**: Interactive contact form for inquiries
- **Professional Styling**: Clean, modern design with a professional color scheme

## Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. No build process or dependencies required - it's a pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript website

## Sections

- **Home**: Hero section with introduction
- **About**: Overview of the research group
- **Team**: Profiles of all team members
- **Research**: Research areas and domains
- **Publications**: Links to Google Scholar profiles
- **Contact**: Contact information and form

## Customization

- **Colors**: Modify CSS variables in `styles.css` (`:root` section)
- **Content**: Edit HTML content in `index.html`
- **Animations**: Adjust JavaScript timing in `script.js`
